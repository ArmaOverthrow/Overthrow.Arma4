**v1.4.0**

⚠️ **SAVE COMPATIBILITY: BREAKING.** Overthrow's save system has been rebuilt from scratch on top of Arma Reforger's own persistence system. Old saves will not load and there is no converter. Back up your save, and if you're not ready to start fresh, pin `1.3.34` in your config.json.

- NEW: Save system rebuilt on Reforger's native persistence (EPF is gone). Characters, gear, vehicles, houses, recruits, jobs and everything you've placed or built are now saved and restored by the game itself
- NEW: Player groups. You can finally squad up. Your group is private by default and named after you; joining someone else's goes through a request/approve prompt that names the other leader and tells you exactly how many recruits you'll bring with you. Your recruits follow you in and out of groups — the leader can command them while you're there, but they stay yours (dismiss, rename, inventory, loadout and XP never transfer) and they come back to you when you leave
- NEW: Rebuilt shop screen. Category tabs, and a proper Sell mode that browses your own inventory instead of making you guess what a shop will take, with greyed-out entries explaining why something can't be sold
- Added: "Sell All" action on vehicle trunks when parked at a shop
- Added: Propaganda posters and a pirate radio transmitter, plus new jobs to put them to use
- Added: Sabotage Radio Tower job
- Added: Assassinate Officer job
- Added: Warehouse "Take All", money shown with thousands separators, and a money change ticker on the HUD
- Changed: Difficulty preset tuning pass

**v1.4.0 fixes (1/6) — Multiplayer & dedicated servers**

- Fixed: Players were never given a faction or a group on dedicated servers — no AI commanding, an empty group menu, and recruits that stayed in their own civilian group (#147)
- Fixed: Group names were never displayed, and there was no way to leave a group once you were in one
- Fixed: Officers could not promote anyone to officer on a dedicated server
- Fixed: Renaming a recruit only changed the name on your own screen
- Fixed: Remove mode for placed and built items did nothing for clients on a dedicated server
- Fixed: Returning players were missing their loadout after a server restart
- Fixed: Player-owned vehicles could disappear on reconnect after a server restart
- Fixed: Your body and your locked vehicles were only held for a few minutes after you logged out. Come back later and you got a fresh character with no gear and a rebuilt, empty vehicle. Both are now held for as long as you're away, across restarts
- Fixed: Difficulty settings were never sent to clients — fast travel costs, build ranges, QRF thresholds and disguise detection distances were all wrong on any server not running Normal

=== MESSAGE 4 ===
**v1.4.0 fixes (3/6) — Exploits & cheat protection**

A large sweep of actions the server used to simply trust the client on have been moved server-side, where they're now validated and charged properly. Previously, a modified client on a dedicated server could:

- Fixed: Mint unlimited money by selling, or through the resistance funds transfer
- Fixed: Claim any building for free, evict any renter and fabricate warehouse stock
- Fixed: Instantly capture any base, or start a QRF anywhere on the map
- Fixed: Level any player's skills for free (and crash the server by levelling past the cap)
- Fixed: Spawn any item in the game into a vehicle at base price
- Fixed: Flip every village on the map with a single supporter conversion
- Fixed: Delete other players' saved loadouts, or equip a full saved kit for free
- Fixed: Get garrisons, structures, placeables and recruits without paying — including recruiting enemy soldiers
- Fixed: Lock or unlock any vehicle on the server, including other players'
- Fixed: Crash the server outright with out-of-range values sent from a number of menus

Also fixed: duplicating a weapon and all of its attachments endlessly at an equipment box — that one was reachable in completely normal play, no modified client needed.
