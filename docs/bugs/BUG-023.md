**Found by:** economy epic discovery (`/discover-feature economy/market` + `economy/real-estate`, 2026-08-02).

`OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.UpdateRents()` (`OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.c:225-270`, the midnight pass of the economic clock) iterates `realEstate.m_mRenters`, paying owners, charging tenants, and evicting anyone who cannot pay. Both the `isResistanceOwned` and `isResistanceRented` branches use `return` where they mean `continue` (`:243-256`), aborting the **entire loop** instead of skipping the one property.

**Impact:** the first resistance-owned or resistance-rented property in map iteration order silently stops rent processing for every remaining renter that night — no rent income for owners, no rent charges or evictions for tenants. Since map order is effectively arbitrary, the symptom is a flaky, hard-to-reproduce "rent sometimes doesn't happen" once the resistance owns or rents anything. Zero test coverage of `UpdateRents`; a Logic-tier case with one resistance property followed by one normal renter would catch it.
