**Found by:** occupying epic discovery (`/discover-feature occupying/qrf`, 2026-08-02).

`OVT_QRFControllerComponent.SendTroops()` budgets a battle from the faction's war chest but never charges for it:

- `:222` — `resources = m_OccupyingFaction.m_iResources` is **read** (then floored at 400 "emergency resources" and capped at `maxQRF × player-count ladder`), but `m_iResources` is never decremented anywhere in the QRF lifecycle.
- `:25`, `:294` — `m_iUsedResources` is accumulated per spawned group and then **discarded**; nothing reads it.
- `m_iResourcesLeft` is a local wave-allocation counter, not a debit.

Combined with the 400 floor, every QRF is unconditionally free and never smaller than a minimum size — the faction can fight unlimited full-scale battles while broke, and player pressure on the OF economy (the epic's core strategic loop) has no effect on its ability to counter-attack.

Fix shape: debit `m_iResources` by the actually-allocated amount when `SendTroops()`/`SendWave()` commits it (or on battle end via `m_iUsedResources`, which already tracks the right number). Decide interaction with the "emergency resources" floor deliberately (e.g. allow going to 0 but not negative). World-free Logic-tier candidate once the budget math is injectable.

**Impact:** gameplay/balance — severs the intended link between the resource economy and the faction's combat power; `maxQRF` difficulty tuning only shapes wave size, never affordability.
