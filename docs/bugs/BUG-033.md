**Found by:** skills epic discovery (`/discover-feature skills/influence`, 2026-08-02).

Every guard on port importing lives in client UI: `OVT_VehicleMenuContext.Import()` checks `HasPermission("Import")` (`OVT_VehicleMenuContext.c:170-189`) and 20m port proximity; `OVT_PortContext` widens the catalogue only when `HasPermission("IllegalImports")` (`OVT_PortContext.c:56-109`).

The server RPC re-checks **none of it**. `RpcAsk_ImportToVehicle` (`OVT_PlayerCommsComponent.c:473-500`) validates neither permission, nor port proximity, nor that the requested item is importable at all — it charges base price (`GetPrice(id)`, no discount) and spawns the prefabs straight into the nearest vehicle's storage.

A crafted client can therefore import **any** arsenal-catalogue item — including occupying-faction gear that even `IllegalImports` shouldn't reach, since the legal/illegal split is purely a client-side catalogue filter — with no Trade skill, anywhere on the map.

Fix shape: in the RPC, resolve the sender's `OVT_PlayerData` and require `HasPermission("Import")`; for items outside the standard shop-type catalogue require `HasPermission("IllegalImports")`; reject occupying-faction items outright; re-check port proximity server-side. (Related product gap, not part of this bug: imports also ignore the Trade discount the skill advertises — `:485`, `:499` use raw `GetPrice`.)

**Impact:** complete bypass of the Trade skill's gating and the port economy in multiplayer — free-form item spawning at base price for any client. Same client-authority class as BUG-020/BUG-021 (economy) and BUG-025 (occupying).
