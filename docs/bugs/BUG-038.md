**Found by:** jobs epic discovery (`/discover-feature jobs/core`, 2026-08-02).

`OVT_JobManagerComponent.RpcDo_UpdateJob` upserts into the client's `m_aJobs` by matching `jobIndex` + `townId`/`baseId` only. Player-allocated jobs (`!m_bPublic`) can legitimately exist once **per player** in the same town — two players standing in the same town each get their own `findGunDealer` (jobIndex 2) instance. On every other client (and on each other's clients), the second `RpcDo_UpdateJob` overwrites the first player's record instead of inserting a second one: same index, same town, different owner — but owner isn't part of the key.

`RpcDo_RemoveJob` **does** include `owner` in its match, so update and remove disagree on job identity: a record upserted under the wrong owner can then fail to match its own removal, leaving a phantom entry until the next full `RplLoad`.

Client-visible symptoms: another player's job replacing yours in the jobs menu (the menu filters by owner, so your job silently disappears), stale/phantom cards, and `m_vCurrentWaypoint`/detail panes pointing at someone else's instance.

Fix shape: include `owner` in `RpcDo_UpdateJob`'s parameters and match logic so update and remove share one identity tuple (`jobIndex`, `townId`, `baseId`, `owner`) — mirroring what `RpcAsk_AcceptJob`/`RpcAsk_DeclineJob` already resolve server-side.

**Impact:** any server with ≥2 players near the same town corrupts the replicated job board for player-allocated jobs; compounds BUG-037 (once that's fixed, simultaneous tutorial jobs become the common case and hit this immediately).
