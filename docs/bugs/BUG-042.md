**Found by:** resistance epic discovery (`/discover-feature resistance/loadouts`, 2026-08-02).

The loadout system's conservation design — "never spawn, consume matching items from the box" — is bypassed for exactly the most valuable item class. `OVT_LoadoutManagerComponent.ApplyLoadoutInternal` routes `m_bIsEquipped` items to `ApplyEquippedItem` (`OVT_LoadoutManagerComponent.c:470-473`), which conjures the weapon via `SpawnEntityPrefab` (`:1278`) instead of `FindItemInBox`. Attachments are also spawned fresh (`:1854-1895`), and nested box items get spawned attachments too (`:1633-1636`). The box's own copy of the weapon is never consumed.

Repro (normal play, no cheating): save a loadout while holding an expensive rifle → apply it repeatedly at any placed Equipment Box ($80 placeable) → one free rifle + attachments per apply.

**Impact:** infinite item duplication and total economy bypass, reachable by any honest player.

Fix shape: route equipped items through `FindItemInBox` + consume-or-fail like every other item; only re-apply attachments actually transferred from the box.
