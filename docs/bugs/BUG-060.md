**Found by:** towns epic discovery (`/discover-feature towns/core` + `towns/stability`, 2026-08-02). Same client-trust class as BUG-034 (skills) and the resistance epic's RPC findings.

1. **Server side:** `RpcAsk_AddStabilityModifier` (`OVT_TownManagerComponent.c:1399-1414`) and `RpcAsk_AddSupportModifier` (`:1421-1435`) index `m_Towns[townId]` and `system.m_Config.m_aModifiers[index]` straight from RPC arguments — no bounds check, no stack-limit re-check, no authority check. A crafted client can send an out-of-range index to crash the server, or spam index 8 (`RecentBattle`, −50, non-stackable but timer-refreshing) onto every town, flooring stability campaign-wide (tax, stock, population growth all scale with it).
2. **Client side:** all nine `RpcDo_*` handlers (`:1441-1577`) do `m_Towns[townId]` with no guard, and `RplLoad` (`:1357`) dereferences `GetTown(i)` against the *server's* town count with no bounds check. Any client whose world query produced a different town list (mod/world mismatch) gets out-of-range access or silent writes to the wrong town.

**Additional note:** `RpcAsk_AddSupportModifier` also omits the recalculation its stability twin performs — an added support modifier has no effect until the next 70 s support pass, and one that expires within a window has no effect at all (fold into the fix or track separately when touched).

**Fix shape:** bounds-validate `townId`/`index` + re-check stack limits in both `RpcAsk_` handlers; guard the `RpcDo_` handlers and `RplLoad`; consider a town-count checksum in the JIP stream.
