**Found by:** towns epic discovery (`/discover-feature towns/support` + `towns/stability` + `towns/core`, 2026-08-02).

`OVT_BaseUpgradeTownPatrol.c:53-59` (the legacy base-upgrade patrol path — still the live static-defense system per the occupying epic):

1. **Dead names.** It calls `TryAddByName` with `"RecentPatrolNegative"`, `"RecentPatrolPositive"` (stability system) and `"RecentPatrol"` (support system). None of the three exist in `Configs/Modifiers/stabilityModifiers.conf` or `supportModifiers.conf` — `TryAddByName` returns false, `RemoveByName` returns silently. **All three calls are permanent no-ops**: occupying patrols walking through towns have zero stability/support effect, while the "PatrolSpotted" notification at `:47` still fires, making the feature look alive. (The deployment-driven `PatrolHarassment` is the only surviving patrol effect — itself crippled by BUG-058, and starved long-term by BUG-028's deployment leak.)
2. **Headcount vs percentage.** The gate at `:51` is `if(town.support >= 75)` — raw supporter **count**, four lines after `:44` correctly uses `town.SupportPercentage() > 25`. A 300-pop city with 80 supporters (27%) is treated as hostile; a 40-pop village with 38 supporters (95%) is treated as friendly.

**Fix shape:** decide the intent — either add the three entries to the configs (append-only!) or retarget the calls at the existing `PatrolHarassment` names — and change `:51` to `SupportPercentage() >= 75`. Alternatively, if base-upgrades' patrol path is being retired in favour of deployments (the occupying epic's stalled migration), delete the dead calls instead.
