**Found by:** resistance epic discovery (`/discover-feature resistance/wanted-system`, 2026-08-03).

`OVT_PlayerWantedComponent.CheckEntity` (`:554`, `:558`) early-outs on `GetTargetCount(ETargetCategory.FRIENDLY) == 0` and only ever queries `GetTargetsList(targets, ETargetCategory.FRIENDLY)`. But `CheckWanted` (`:513-525`) reacts to wanted level > 1 by calling `SetPerceivedFactionOverride(playerFaction)` — which moves the character into the AI's **ENEMY** target bucket. Net effect: the moment you become properly wanted, the detection scan can no longer find any AI that has you as a target, so `m_bTempSeen` stays false exactly when you are most visible.

**Impact:** during an active firefight the HUD's seen-eye goes dark and the decay timer (`:462-479`) starts counting down while enemies are actively engaging you. The wanted level is only held up by the server-side combat-event writers (`SCR_AIConfigComponent`, `SCR_CharacterDamageManagerComponent`) re-escalating on each shot/hit — stop firing and hide behind a wall in full view of a squad that is hunting you, and you decay as if unseen. It also breaks level-4 escalation (armed vehicle / Mobile FOB) for already-wanted players, since that path requires the scan to see you.

**Fix shape:** query the ENEMY category as well when `m_iWantedLevel > 1` (or both buckets unconditionally), or track engagement separately (e.g. hold `m_bIsSeen`/pause decay while any nearby OF AI has the owner in any target list).
