**Found by:** resistance epic discovery (`/discover-feature resistance/wanted-system`, 2026-08-03).

Clustered null-safety gaps around `OVT_PlayerWantedComponent`:

- `CheckUpdate` dereferences `m_Percieve.GetVisualRecognitionFactor()` (`:326`) with no null check on the perceivable component.
- `m_Faction.GetAffiliatedFaction()` results are dereferenced immediately at `:339` and `:494` (`CheckWanted`) — a character with no affiliated faction VM-errors the tick.
- `OVT_MapContext.CanFastTravel` (`:75`) casts `FindComponent(OVT_PlayerWantedComponent)` and calls `GetWantedLevel()` on it without checking the cast — any controlled entity lacking the component (e.g. possessing a vehicle or a non-standard character) errors the fast-travel check.
- `OVT_WantedInfo.UpdateSeen` (`:87-95`) uses the found widget `w` without a null check, and `UpdateUndercoverStatus` (`:103-105`) dereferences `OVT_Global.GetConfig()` unguarded; a stray `Print("[Overthrow] WARNING: m_Wanted is null!")` debug line remains at `:150`.

**Impact:** each is a latent VM-error that kills the 1 Hz tick or the HUD update for that character/session; the tick errors are the worst since the `CallLater` keeps firing and spams the log every second.

**Fix shape:** guard-and-return on each resolve; drop the leftover debug print. Mechanical, low-risk — good batch candidate with any other wanted-component change.
