> Filed from the `/discover-feature resistance/fob` code investigation (2026-08-09). Evidence is source-level; not yet reproduced at runtime.

## Symptom

When the undeploy container-collection fails (transfer error path), the deployed FOB entity is deleted but its `OVT_FOBData` record survives forever: a phantom FOB icon on every player's map, a valid fast-travel destination (any FOB within 40 m anchors fast travel, `OVT_MapContext.c:121-123`), and a build/place-permission zone (`MAX_FOB_PLACE_DIS`/`MAX_FOB_BUILD_DIS` 100 m) — all pointing at empty ground. The record persists through saves.

## Root cause

`OVT_ResistanceFactionManager.OnFOBCollectionError` (`Scripts/Game/GameMode/Managers/Factions/OVT_ResistanceFactionManager.c:1770-1810`) deletes the deployed FOB entity ("to prevent it being stuck") and sends `FOBUndeployFailed`, but — unlike the success path `OnFOBCollectionComplete` (`:1751`) — never calls `UnregisterFOB`. The record stays in `m_FOBs`, replicates to clients, and round-trips through `OVT_PersistedFOB`.

This is the surviving sibling of BUG-046 (closed): that fix added validate-before-spawn and 10 m-tolerance unregistration, but the error-path record leak was not part of it.

## Fix (proposed)

In `OnFOBCollectionError`, capture the FOB's origin before `DeleteEntityAndChildren` and call `UnregisterFOB(pos)` — mirroring the success path. Decide deliberately what happens to the spawned truck on this path (it is left in-world with physics still frozen unless the error handler re-activates it — verify while fixing).

## Notes

- The error path also leaves the FOB's garrison AI alive (that half is BUG-125, which applies to the success path too).
- A cheap recovery for already-poisoned saves: `ApplyPersistedResistance` could drop FOB records that have no deployed-FOB vehicle within tolerance — but that risks deleting records for legitimately-despawned vehicles; investigate before adding.
