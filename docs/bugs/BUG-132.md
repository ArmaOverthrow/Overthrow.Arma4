## How this was found

Live repro by the mod owner (2026-08-09): dedicated server (`OverthrowDS`) + two clients. Client 1 drives their own **unlocked** starter UAZ; client 2's "get in" action is disabled with reason **"Hostile"**. Both players unwanted (perceived-civilian). Investigated against source and all three machines' logs (`logs_2026-08-09_15-23-*`). Not previously filed — all 131 bug files checked. **BUG-088** (closed) is the sibling: it fixed a missing `RplComponent` on the same hand-authored prefab and its Related section explicitly warned that other components lost the same way should be hunted. This is the second one.

## Symptom

In any session with replication (dedicated or listen host, remote clients):
- A client cannot enter a vehicle that has any occupant — "get in" disabled, reason "Hostile" — even when both players are same-faction, unwanted, and the vehicle is unlocked and owned by the driver.
- Works when the vehicle is empty (and for the host on a listen server), which is what makes it look intermittent.
- Same cause silently breaks every other client-side `IsFactionEnemy` consumer: vehicle trunk (`SCR_OpenVehicleStorageAction.c:63`) and casualty-removal (`SCR_RemoveCasualtyUserAction.c:83`) on occupied vehicles, support stations, friendly-fire classification, intel faction checks. Nametags mask it (`OVT_NameTagRulesetFaction` compares faction *keys*, not relations).

## Root cause

**The chain (all client-side, where the action's `CanBePerformedScript` runs):**

1. Overthrow's `Scripts/Game/UserActions/Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c:66-77` (a full copy of vanilla `SCR_GetInUserAction.c:87-98`, no `super`) compares `character.GetFaction()` vs `vehicle.GetFaction()` with `IsFactionEnemy` → `#AR-UserAction_SeatHostile`.
2. Both compared factions are **CIV**: `Character_Player.et:168` sets actual affiliation `CIV` (FIA lives only on the PlayerController via `OVT_SpawnLogic.SetCivilianFaction`, and the wanted system's `SetPerceivedFactionOverride` deliberately never touches actual affiliation — `OVT_PlayerWantedComponent.c:543`). The car: `SCR_VehicleFactionAffiliationComponent` clears to null at init (`:12-15`) and **adopts the first occupant's faction** on entry (`:32-47`, engine callback, runs on every machine) — hence the state-dependence: empty car has null faction, check skipped.
3. `CIV.IsFactionEnemy(CIV)` answers **true on clients**: `SCR_Faction.DoCheckIfFactionFriendly` (`SCR_Faction.c:504-507`) reads `m_FriendlyFactions`, and the entire relations init — including self-friendly (`m_bFriendlyToSelf`, applied `:766-768`) — is **server-gated** (`InitFactionRelationships`, gate at `:753`). Clients receive relations **only** via faction delegates: `SCR_FactionManager.SetFactionsFriendly` (`SCR_FactionManager.c:591-637`) silently no-ops without them (`:623-630`); JIP ships the friendly matrix through `SCR_EditableFactionComponent.RplSave` (`:494-550`). `SCR_FactionManager`'s own RplSave carries no relations (`SCR_Faction.c:948-968` — tasks flag + player limit only).
4. **`Prefabs/GameMode/OVT_OverthrowFactionManager.et` has no `SCR_DelegateFactionManagerComponent`** — its only component is the `RplComponent` BUG-088 added. Vanilla `FactionManager_Base.et` carries `SCR_DelegateFactionManagerComponent` (delegate prefab `{94D481BAEC3B8573}PrefabsEditable/System/E_FactionDelegate.et`) plus `Persistence`. The prefab was hand-authored rather than derived from `FactionManager_Base.et` — exactly how BUG-088's component went missing. (Overthrow's older, unused `OVT_FactionManager.et:4` still has the component.) All three worlds instantiate the broken prefab (`OVT_Campaign_Eden_Layers/managers.layer:272`, `OVT_Campaign_Test_Layers/default.layer:44`, `OVT_TestIsland_Layers/Overthrow.layer:12`).

**Net effect:** zero `FactionDelegate` entities in any of the three logs → every client's `m_FriendlyFactions` set is permanently empty → **every faction is hostile to every faction, including itself** — on clients only. The server evaluates `CIV vs CIV = friendly`, which is why nothing server-side ever flagged it.

**Why get-in specifically:** vanilla defends other vehicle actions against self-hostile factions (`SCR_OpenVehicleDoorUserAction.c:89`, `SCR_ToggleHandbrakeAction.c:54` add `&& vehicleFaction != characterFaction`, with a comment about FFA/mods; `SCR_VehicleFactionAffiliationComponent.c:57` guards its auto-eject the same way — why client 1 isn't ejected from his own car). `SCR_GetInUserAction` — vanilla and Overthrow's copy — lacks the guard.

## Log evidence (session 2026-08-09 15:23)

- DS `console.log:164` creates `OVT_OverthrowFactionManager`; **zero** `FactionDelegate`/`PrefabsEditable/System` lines in all three logs.
- `SetCivilianFaction: player 1 -> FIA … accepted=true` / `player 2 -> FIA` (BUG-088's fix working — controller faction only).
- Both clients log the same door events at 15:24:25 (client 2's machine processed the compartment entry, so its local affiliation component adopted CIV).
- No wanted/disguise activity on any machine.

## Fix (proposed)

1. **The fix (one line, mirrors BUG-088):** add to `Prefabs/GameMode/OVT_OverthrowFactionManager.et`:
   ```
   SCR_DelegateFactionManagerComponent "<fresh id>" {
     m_FactionDelegatePrefab "{94D481BAEC3B8573}PrefabsEditable/System/E_FactionDelegate.et"
   }
   ```
2. **Belt-and-braces (also a real vanilla defect):** add `&& vehicleFaction != characterFaction` to `Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c:72`, matching the door/handbrake actions.
3. **Longer-term:** re-parent the prefab from `FactionManager_Base.et` (also restores `Persistence` and `m_DefaultRanks`); audit remaining hand-authored replacements of vanilla manager prefabs (BUG-088's Related item, still open in spirit).

## Fix implemented and verified (2026-08-09)

- `Prefabs/GameMode/OVT_OverthrowFactionManager.et` — added `SCR_DelegateFactionManagerComponent` (local id `{6B1C4E7A0D3F5199}`) with vanilla's delegate prefab `{94D481BAEC3B8573}E_FactionDelegate.et`, mirroring `FactionManager_Base.et` and the BUG-088 fix pattern.
- `Scripts/Game/UserActions/Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c` — added the `vehicleFaction != characterFaction` same-faction guard vanilla carries on the door/handbrake actions (belt-and-braces; also a real vanilla omission).
- New Init-tier case `OVT_TEST_Init_FactionDelegates_Spawn` — asserts `SCR_DelegateFactionManagerComponent.GetInstance()` resolves and holds exactly one delegate per faction. **Proven red by reproducing the literal defect** (component block removed from the prefab → "GetInstance() is null"), then green restored. Its pass is also the positive runtime confirmation that delegates spawn — entity-creation logging never shows them.
- Compile-check clean; full All group **76/76 green**.
- **Play-test verified by the mod owner (2026-08-09)**: dedicated server + 2 clients restarted on the fixed build — client 2 can now enter the vehicle client 1 is driving.

Deferred (tech debt, not this fix): re-parent the prefab from `FactionManager_Base.et` (would also restore `Persistence`/`m_DefaultRanks`); audit other hand-authored replacements of vanilla manager prefabs; refresh the stale vanilla copy in `Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c` (missing loitering/supplies/`IsReserved` checks that vanilla has grown since).

## Confirming experiments (live session, before any change)

1. Client 2 → **empty** car: allowed (null vehicle faction). Any occupant enters → "Hostile". Proves adopt-then-compare.
2. Swap roles (client 2 drives, client 1 approaches): still "Hostile" — kills any join-order theory.
3. Listen host: host may enter a client-occupied car; the remote client may not — proves it's the client-side relation table.
4. Fingerprint: with client 1 inside, client 2's **trunk** action blocked, but **door open/close** and **handbrake** work (those carry the self-hostility guard).

## Notes

- Hypotheses tested and rejected: wanted/disguise changing actual faction (perceived-only by design, both players unwanted, no log activity); BUG-088 JIP gap (comparison is X-vs-X, not an index mismatch; both clients equally broken regardless of join order); `SetVehicleAsEnemy` (fires only on wanted-level-4 armed-vehicle/Mobile-FOB paths; car is a civilian UAZ).
- Tech debt spotted in the same file: `Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c` `CanBePerformedScript` is a stale full copy of vanilla — it has drifted and now lacks vanilla's loitering, supplies-occupied, `IsGetInAndOutBlockedByDoorUser` and `IsReserved` checks (`SCR_GetInUserAction.c:68-117`).
- Expected collateral repaired by the fix: all client-side `IsFactionEnemy` consumers listed under Symptom.
