## How this was found

Found while **implementing** the gun-dealer price carets the user asked for (`map/location-types` Phase 6, 2026-08-10): the carets came out at ▲▲▲ for every weapon at every dealer, which prompted tracing the term back to its inputs. Recorded as finding **N19** and verified independently by the run's orchestrator before the feature shipped. **Re-verified against the working tree 2026-08-11.**

This is the reason `m_bShowWeaponCarets` ships **default-off** on the gun-dealer map panel — see "Live consequence" below.

## Symptom

Two, one economic and one visible:

1. **Gun-dealer prices never move.** Every item a gun dealer sells carries the full +10% scarcity markup at all times. Buying the town out, or selling a crate of magazines back, changes nothing — the price the dealer quotes is the same on day 1 and day 40. Ordinary shops respond to stock; gun dealers cannot.
2. **Price carets on the gun-dealer map panel read ▲▲▲ for every weapon, everywhere** — which looks like "this dealer is a rip-off" and is not a per-dealer fact at all. Four identical maximum-scarcity rows carry zero information and actively mislead.

Note it is **not** just weapons: pistols, ammunition, attachments, throwables and explosives are all dealer-only too, so the whole dealer inventory is affected.

## Root cause

The price expression has two terms (`Scripts/Game/GameMode/Managers/OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.c:567`):

```
price = Math.Round(price
      + ((1 - ((float)stock_level / max_stock)) * (price * 0.1))   // scarcity, bounded +-10%
      + (price * distance_to_port * 0.0001));                      // remoteness
```

with `stock_level = GetTownStock(townID, id)` (`:563`). For any gun-dealer item that value is **always 0**, for two independent reasons — either alone is sufficient:

**1. The dealer's own stock is not town stock.** `GetTownStock` sums over `m_mTownShops[townId]` (`:603-609`). That map is populated only by `CheckShopInit` (`:1806-1811`), the callback of the world-init shop scan. A gun dealer is not found by that scan — it is spawned later, by `OVT_TownController.SpawnGunDealer()` (`:233`), and registered through `RegisterGunDealer` (`:1257-1263`), which inserts into `m_aGunDealers` and **nothing else**. So a dealer's inventory is invisible to every town-stock query, including the one pricing its own goods.

**2. No town shop stocks a weapon anyway.** `Configs/System/ShopConfig.conf` defines four shop types whose rules cover only `EQUIPMENT` (map, binoculars, watch, general), `HEAL`, `BACKPACK`, `HEADWEAR`, `TORSO`, `VEST_AND_WAIST`, `LEGS` and `FOOTWEAR`. No `WEAPON`-mode or weapon-type rule exists at any town shop.

With `stock_level = 0` and `max_stock` clamped to at least 1 (`:565`), the scarcity term is `(1 − 0/max) × price × 0.1` — **exactly +10%, the ceiling, permanently**. And because the player's purchases only ever move the dealer's own `m_aInventory`, nothing the player does can change the input.

The remoteness term is unaffected and does vary between dealers, so it remains meaningful.

## Live consequence (do not remove this without reading it)

`map/location-types` Decision 8: the gun-dealer panel's weapon price carets are implemented and working, but gated behind **`m_bShowWeaponCarets`, defaulting off** (`Scripts/Game/UI/Map/LocationTypes/Shop/OVT_MapShopPriceIndicator.c` and the Shop/GunDealer entries in `Configs/Map/OverthrowMap.conf`). One conf line turns them on. That flag is off **because of this bug** and for no other reason — turning it on without fixing this restores four permanently-misleading ▲▲▲ rows on every dealer panel in the game. The remoteness badge is unaffected and still renders.

## Fix (proposed)

Decide what a gun dealer's price is supposed to respond to. Three options, in increasing scope:

1. **Register dealers into their town's shop list.** `RegisterGunDealer` also inserts into `m_mTownShops[townId]` (the town id is already resolved in `SpawnGunDealer`, `OVT_TownController.c:281-285`, and written to `shop.m_iTownId`). Then a dealer's own stock is its town's stock for weapons, and buying weapons raises their price exactly as it does for medical supplies. **Smallest change, and it is what the pricing model already assumes.** Check the blast radius first: `m_mTownShops` also drives restock (`:294-320`), the buy cap (`:641`) and the town-shop iteration at `:373` and `:417` — a dealer appearing there must be correct for all of them, and `FilterShopEntities` deliberately excludes dealers elsewhere (`:863`).
2. **Give town shops weapon rules** in `ShopConfig.conf` so weapons genuinely have a town-wide supply. Bigger design change — it puts guns on ordinary shelves.
3. **Exempt dealer-only items from the scarcity term** and price them on remoteness alone. Honest, but it silently makes a second pricing model.

Whichever is chosen, re-enable `m_bShowWeaponCarets` in the same change and confirm on screen that a spread of caret levels actually appears — the flag exists to be turned back on.

## Notes

- **Verified twice, independently**, before being written up: once while implementing the carets, once by the orchestrator re-deriving it from the config and the registration path.
- The map panel deliberately computes town stock with its own null-safe loop rather than calling `GetTownStock` (BUG-140), so fixing this bug changes what the panel *displays* but not how it reads.
- No automated gate can see this: the arithmetic is correct, the config is well-formed, and every test passes. It is only visible if you ask what the numbers *mean*.
