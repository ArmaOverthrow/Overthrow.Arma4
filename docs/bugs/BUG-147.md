## Symptom

Observed in play 2026-08-13 (during BUG-146 verification): **unarmed** recruits follow orders and reach (almost) the ordered position, but walk backwards nearly the whole way. Armed recruits move normally.

## FALSIFIED: the UNKNOWN-target stare theory

First hypothesis was that the recruit stares at occupying soldiers (neither friendly nor — while unarmed — enemy → UNKNOWN category → look reaction) while walking away. **Play-test 2026-08-13 killed it**: with the map cleared of every enemy, an unarmed recruit still walks backwards and is visibly not looking at anyone or anything. Whatever pins the facing is not a perception target.

## Probe data (2026-08-13, empty map, unarmed recruit moonwalking)

```
affil=FIA perceived=CIV wanted=0 inHands=false hasWeapon=false aiActive=true
ussrEnemy=false group=-1/FIA threat=SAFE action=SCR_AIMoveInFormationBehavior target=none
```

Player observation: he fixates on a direction (varies per occurrence, sticky once acquired, nothing there) and backpedals/strafes to keep facing it. Ambient civilians — unarmed but moving on ordinary waypoint moves, never formation-following — do NOT moonwalk. **The suspect is therefore `SCR_AIMoveInFormationBehavior`'s facing/aiming handling on a weaponless character** — vanilla never ships unarmed formation members, so a stale body-aim direction that armed characters' weapon-handling would clear could persist indefinitely here. Investigation of the formation BT's aim nodes is in progress.

## ACTUAL trigger found by play-test (2026-08-13, second iteration): possession release

The real reproducer: **the "Open Inventory" recruit command** — implemented as a brief possession (`SetPossessedEntity(target)` → open inventory → on close `SetPossessedEntity(null)`). A freshly recruited civilian walks and looks around normally; **open + close their inventory once and their gaze locks forever** (wherever the player was looking at release). Likely affects armed recruits too — the earlier "armed = fine" observation came from a save whose recruits had not been possessed that session. This also falsifies the formation-behavior theory below as the *trigger* (the yaw-pinning mechanics remain valid as the *expression*): the modded `SCR_AIMoveInFormationBehavior` look-gate was removed again.

**Mechanism:** while possessed, player input drives the character's aiming; on release nothing anywhere resets it (vanilla `SCR_PossessingManagerComponent` is pure bookkeeping — no controller cleanup), and the AI's only aim-stop (`AITaskCharacterStop` in `LookAction.bt`) never fires for an aim it did not start. The stale player aim pins the body yaw; locomotion backpedals.

**Fix iteration 2 (insufficient):** `OVT_Global.ResetAIAimState(character)` — clears weapon-raised, forced freelook, persistent states, zeroes the `AimingComponent` rotation, resets `SCR_AILookAction` (`Cancel()` + `Complete()`); called server-side after `SetPossessedEntity(null)` in both restore handlers. Play-test: still moonwalks — the reset likely runs BEFORE the still-live inventory menu finishes tearing down and re-damages the state.

**Vanilla exonerated:** GM-possessing a vanilla character, unarming it and releasing produces normal movement (play-tested) — vanilla never unpossesses under a live menu. RFG-010 withdrawn accordingly; the defect is Overthrow's flow.

**Fix iteration 3 (implemented 2026-08-13, VERIFIED IN PLAY):** the close event fires at menu-teardown START, so Overthrow was restoring possession under a still-live inventory menu. `OVT_PlayerCommsComponent.OnClientInventoryStateChanged` now (a) one-shots its invoker subscription (previously every Open Inventory stacked another), and (b) defers `RequestRestorePossession` by 300 ms (`RequestRestorePossessionDeferred`) so the menu is fully gone before possession flips back — reproducing the exact situation vanilla GM release handles cleanly. `ResetAIAimState` stays in the restore handlers as belt-and-braces.

## Superseded analysis: formation-move facing mechanics (kept — the yaw mechanics are real, the trigger attribution was wrong)

The look/aim system (`LookAction.bt`) runs **permanently in parallel** with every behavior (`Soldier.bt:196-199`), fed by `SCR_AILookAtThreatSystemLogic` → `SCR_AISectorThreatFilter`: **averaged threat-sector estimates with 45 s memory**, refreshed by mundane danger events (doors, horns, physics contacts) — exactly "staring at nothing, sticky, direction varies per occurrence". An active aim request pins the character's yaw. **With a weapon in hands the weapon/torso aim layer absorbs that yaw** and the body keeps facing the movement direction; with empty hands it is realized as full-body heading, and locomotion resolves as backpedaling toward the formation slot.

`KeepInFormation.bt` (the tree behind `SCR_AIMoveInFormationBehavior`, `SCR_AIMoveBehavior.c:21-46`) is uniquely defenseless: unlike `MoveIndividually.bt` (which re-establishes upper-body state on entry — why unarmed *civilians on waypoint moves* never moonwalk) it never resets aim state and never issues the aim-stop (`AITaskCharacterStop "Stop flags" 2` exists in exactly one tree in all of vanilla: `LookAction.bt:23/:190/:264`, gated on the current behavior's `m_bAllowLook`). It even requests `OrientationType ToPoint` with no point connected (`KeepInFormation.bt:258-265`) — the only vanilla move node in that state.

## Removed: the formation-look gate (wrong-target fix, deleted)

A modded `SCR_AIMoveInFormationBehavior` briefly shipped `m_bAllowLook = false` for empty-handed followers, built on the formation theory above. Deleted once the possession trigger was found — fresh unpossessed recruits never moonwalked, so it addressed a phantom and only diverged us from vanilla.
