## Symptom

Ordering a recruit to "Move here" never lands them on the ordered spot — they stop meters away, even with a single recruit in the group. Placing a recruit at a specific defensive position or next to a specific ammo box is effectively impossible. (Reported 2026-08-13 after the BUG-146/147 arc.)

## Mechanism (traced through vanilla 1.7 source)

The ordered position itself is **exact** (pure camera raycast, `SCR_PlayerControllerCommandingComponent.PhysicsCommandTrace`, no navmesh projection). All error is downstream:

1. **`SCR_WaypointGroupCommand.m_fCompletionRadius` defaults to 5 m** (`SCR_WaypointGroupCommand.c:17-18`) and is stamped onto the spawned waypoint at runtime (`:98`) — editing `AIWaypoint_Move.et`'s own radius does nothing.
2. **`CompletionType Any`**: the waypoint auto-completes the moment ANY member enters the 5 m bubble (XZ distance, group-level test), which cancels the whole move activity — everyone stops wherever they are.
3. The group move task's precision is **hardcoded 1.0 m** in `ActivityMove.bt:19-20` (BT asset, not script-tunable) — so even without early completion, ~1 m is the convergence floor.
4. After completion the slave group holds loosely (`Idle_ReturnToFormation.bt`, 2 m threshold; 2+ members re-form Wedge on the slave leader) — they do NOT resume following the player, so holding position works; only the placement accuracy is broken.

## Fix shipped (Lever A, 2026-08-13): completion radius 5 → 1

`Configs/Commanding/Commands.conf` is a same-GUID delta of vanilla's — added delta blocks for `waypointMove` (`{5A16938165F68AF8}`) and `waypointMoveForced` (`{5A16938165F68AC6}`) setting `m_fCompletionRadius 1`. Single-recruit orders should now land within ~1 m (the BT precision floor). Deliberately did NOT force-flag the normal Move (priority 1000 would make moving recruits ignore combat; the forced variant exists for that).

Known limits of Lever A:
- **Multi-recruit orders are still imprecise**: first arriver completes the waypoint for everyone, and survivors re-form in Wedge around the slave leader.
- If the AI's own precision stalls just outside 1 m, the waypoint may stay alive (they keep micro-adjusting — acceptable for "stand here", and any subsequent order clears previous waypoints).

## Remaining work (Lever B, designed, not built): `OVT_MoveToExactCommand`

Per-agent exact placement + hold, following `OVT_OpenInventoryCommand`'s patterns:
- `Execute()` server-side: clear the slave group's waypoints (as `SCR_WaypointGroupCommand.c:65-70`), then per agent: `new SCR_AIMoveIndividuallyBehavior(utility, null, pos, PRIORITY_BEHAVIOR_MOVE, PRIORITY_LEVEL_PLAYER, null, 0.5)` + `utility.WrapBehaviorOutsideOfVehicle()` + `AddAction()` — radius maps 1:1 to `AITaskMove` PrecisionXZ; 0.5 is vanilla-proven (`SCR_DefenderSpawnerComponent.c:346`).
- On arrival (`m_OnActionCompleted`): pin a `SCR_AIDefendBehavior(utility, null, wp, exactPos, angularRange, PRIORITY_BEHAVIOR_DEFEND, PRIORITY_LEVEL_PLAYER)` so `Idle_ReturnToFormation.bt` (2 m threshold) can't drag them back to formation, and they get facing/stance for free.
- Defensive `GetClosestPositionOnNavmesh(pos, "2 2 2", pos)` before dispatch — an off-navmesh point (on a crate, inside a shed) strands the AI at the nearest reachable node with no feedback.
- Registration: class entry in `Configs/Commanding/Commands.conf` + menu entry in `Configs/Commanding/CommandingMenu.conf`.
