## Symptom (observed on gamepad, 2026-08-13)

While positioning a placeable: pressing **A** places the item and **jumps**; **d-pad Right** (next item) also **opens the inventory**; pressing **Y** (rotate right) repeatedly also triggers the **double-tap weapon-category switch** and equips a weapon. Every place-mode verb double-fires its vanilla character meaning.

## Mechanism

`OverthrowPlaceContext` (Overthrow `chimeraInputCommon.conf`) is declared at **Priority 10, Flags 2**, and `OVT_PlaceContext.OnFrame` re-activates it per frame while placing or in removal mode — the activation machinery is correct. But vanilla's entire character tier (`CharacterGeneralContext`, `CharacterMovementContext`, `CharacterWeaponContext`) ALSO sits at **Priority 10**, and equal-priority contexts do not shadow each other. The colliding vanilla actions on the claimed inputs, all in that tier:

- `gamepad0:a` → `CharacterJump` (click), `CharacterLean/StanceModifier` (hold) — vs our `MenuSelect` (place)
- `gamepad0:x` → `CharacterReload` (HoldOnce), `PerformAction` — vs `OverthrowRotateLeft`
- `gamepad0:y` → `CharacterNextWeapon` (single-click), `SwitchWeaponCategory3` (**double-click** — the equip the user hit by mashing rotate), `CharacterAction` — vs `OverthrowRotateRight`
- `gamepad0:pad_right` → `Inventory` (click), `GadgetSelection`, `OpenCommandMenu` (hold), `OpenEmoteMenu` — vs `OverthrowNextItem`
- `gamepad0:pad_left` → `CharacterNextMuzzle`, `CharacterNextFireMode`, `CharacterWeaponBipod`, `CharacterWeaponManualSafety` — vs `OverthrowPrevItem`
- `gamepad0:b` → vanilla stance/back handling — vs `MenuBack` (cancel)

## Fix shape — vanilla already solved this exact problem

**`CharacterPlacementContext` is the precedent: Priority 45, Flags 0xa**, activated per frame from `SCR_ItemPlacementComponent` while a placement preview exists — "character still in the world, placement re-claims inputs" is its whole design. `EditorPlacingContext` (Priority 50, Flags 4) is the precedent for re-claiming A/B specifically.

Raise `OverthrowPlaceContext` to **Priority 45** (or 46 to sit above vanilla placement). Because a higher-priority context's action claims its input outright (the BUG-154/F6 mechanism, working for us this time), the six refs it already carries then suppress jump, reload, weapon swap/equip, inventory, and the pad_left weapon-manipulation cluster while placing — the user's requested "disable all vanilla bindings apart from move and aim" for every currently-colliding input, with **zero script changes**. Move (left stick) and aim (right stick / triggers) are untouched because no ref binds them.

Verify-time cautions:
- **Keyboard side effects of the raised refs**: `MenuBack` carries `KC_ESCAPE` and `MenuSelect` may carry Space/Enter — confirm escape still reaches the pause menu from place mode, and that keyboard placement still works.
- **RT still fires the held weapon while placing** (`CharacterWeaponFire`, right_trigger, tier 10) — not reported, and suppressing it would need a new inline no-op action on `right_trigger` inside the context. Decide during the fix whether "aim stays live" includes "fire stays live".
- `pad_up`/`pad_down` gadget quick-slots (flashlight, compass double-click, binoculars hold) also stay live; adding `MenuUp`/`MenuDown` refs to grab them is **NOT safe** — those actions also bind `KC_W`/`KC_S` and would freeze keyboard movement in place mode.
- Same raise should be evaluated for `OverthrowBuildContext` (Priority 50 already) and removal mode, which shares `OnFrame`'s activation.
