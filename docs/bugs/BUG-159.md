## Symptom (observed on gamepad, 2026-08-13)

Opening the place menu triggers the place tutorial tip; while it shows, the place menu cannot be driven with the d-pad — the player has to wait for the tip to time out before selecting anything.

## Mechanism, two halves

1. **Focus is taken and never returned.** `OVT_TutorialContext.FocusFirstButton()` calls `workspace.SetFocusedWidget` on the tip's Next/Dismiss button — deliberately, so a pad player can dismiss a tip shown over open gameplay. But it runs unconditionally, including when a menu already holds focus, and `CloseLayout` restores nothing: after the tip closes (or times out) the menu's focus is gone, not merely interrupted.
2. **The tip shows over the place menu at all, despite a mechanism meant to stop it.** `OVT_PlaceContext.IsBlockingPopups()` returns true on `m_bIsActive || m_bPlacing || m_bRemovalMode` precisely so tips don't fight placement. A tip triggered BY the menu opening evidently evaluates before `m_bIsActive` is set (or the blocking check happens only at trigger time and the open event races it) — undiagnosed; instrument the trigger path to confirm the ordering.

## Fix shape

- In `FocusFirstButton`, capture `workspace.GetFocusedWidget()` first and restore it in `CloseLayout` (null-guarded — the previous widget may be gone). That alone fixes the stranding for every tip over every menu.
- Decide the display policy for tips triggered by menu opens: either defer them until the menu closes (extend the `IsBlockingPopups` contract to cover the open race), or show them without taking focus when a menu context is active (focus theft is only needed over open gameplay, where nothing else holds focus).
- Regression check: a tip over plain gameplay must still take focus, or a pad player cannot dismiss it at all — that is why the steal exists.
