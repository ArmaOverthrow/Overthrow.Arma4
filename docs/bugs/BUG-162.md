## Symptom

Donating to the resistance, sending resistance funds to a player, and sending your own money to a
player all move the money correctly, but **no notification is ever shown to anybody** — the donor's
name never appears, and the recipient is never told they were paid. The three localized strings exist
and are wired in the notification config; they simply never reach a screen.

## Mechanism

Same root cause as BUG-161, in the notification direction.

`OVT_PlayerCommsComponent.SendNotification()` was:

```c
void SendNotification(string tag, int playerId = -1, ...)
{
    Rpc(RpcAsk_SendNotification, tag, playerId, param1, param2, param3);
}
```

— an unconditional `Rpc()` to an `RplRcver.Server` handler. Its **only three callers** were inside
`RpcAsk_DonateToResistance`, `RpcAsk_SendResistanceFunds` and `RpcAsk_SendMoneyToPlayer`, which are
themselves `RplRcver.Server` handlers and therefore already running on the authority. An
`RplRcver.Server` RPC marshalled by the authority is delivered to nobody (BUG-045 / BUG-052 / BUG-088
family), so all three calls were dropped packets.

Note the wrapper was *also* an unvalidated network endpoint: it let any client show any notification,
with any substitutions, to any `playerId`.

## Fix (landed 2026-08-14, Phase 4 of `core/controller-migration`)

The three handlers moved to `OVT_EconomyRequestComponent` on the per-player `OVT_OverthrowController`
and now call `OVT_Global.GetNotify().SendTextNotification(...)` directly. `SendNotification` /
`RpcAsk_SendNotification` are left caller-less on the monolith and are deleted in P8 of the same
migration (implementation.md §3.7 / §4).

## Verification owed

Code-derived verdict; **not yet confirmed at runtime**. The migration's MP play-test (implementation.md
§6, step 8) is the confirming step: donate to the resistance and watch for the `PlayerDonated`
notification.

## Closed 2026-08-14 — also fixed on `main`

On `main`, `SendNotification()` now calls `OVT_Global.GetNotify().SendTextNotification(...)` directly
behind a `Replication.IsServer()` guard, and `RpcAsk_SendNotification` is **deleted** — it had no
client caller and was the unvalidated any-notification-to-any-player endpoint noted above. Confirmed
by play-test: donating to the resistance shows the `PlayerDonated` notification.
