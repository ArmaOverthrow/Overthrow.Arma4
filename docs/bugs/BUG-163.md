Vehicle fast travel should be a special case. We only take people inside that vehicle and for no extra cost, the UI should reflect this and not give the option to bring recruits or not (everyone in the car always goes with the vehicle). You shouldnt need to do anything with the recruits at all, simply teleporting the vehicle should bring all occupants
