## How this was found

Player report 2026-08-15: after finally getting a disguise to resolve (see the FULL_OUTFIT
investigation, BUG-168/169), the player's recruits started shooting at them from range.

## Problem

`OVT_PlayerWantedComponent.CheckWanted` reacted to `m_bIsDisguised` by calling
`CharacterPerceivableComponent.SetPerceivedFactionOverride(occupyingFaction)`. That
override is a single global value consumed by **native** perception bucketing for every
observer: occupying AI moved the player to FRIENDLY (intended), but every player-faction
(FIA) recruit's `PerceptionComponent` moved the player to its **ENEMY** bucket
(FIA vs OF are war enemies).

Enemy engagement is then decided by the native `AIWeaponTargetSelector`
(`SCR_AICombatComponent.EvaluateWeaponAndTarget`), which reads perception targets with
**no script seam** — the modded `SCR_ChimeraAIAgent.IsPerceivedEnemy` "never shoot a
wanted-component carrier" catch-all only gates the four scripted danger *reactions*, not
target selection.

## Why it surfaced only now

Two long-standing conditions masked it:

1. Disguises almost never resolved under `FULL_OUTFIT` (perceived faction all-or-nothing,
   see 2026-08-14 investigation), so the OF override was rarely active near recruits.
2. Pre-BUG-146, never-wanted recruits were hard-coded pacifists who could not construct an
   attack behavior against anyone — including a disguised owner. The BUG-146 fix
   (2026-08-13) correctly let armed recruits fight, which also armed this trap.

Both active and inactive (parked) recruits are affected — anything FIA-faction with
weapons.

## Fix

`Scripts/Game/Components/Player/OVT_PlayerWantedComponent.c` (`CheckWanted`): while
disguised and not wanted, present **CIV** instead of the occupying faction. Nothing is
lost: occupying AI already treat CIV as non-hostile (identical to the undisguised-clean
state), the OF detection scan reads the FRIENDLY bucket where CIV-presenting players
already live, and the real disguise mechanics — base-proximity wanted immunity and the
15 m `CheckDisguisedAsOccupying` blow check — key off `m_bIsDisguised` in this component,
not off the override. Wanted ≥ 2 still presents the player faction (hostile) as before.

## Play-test

Wear a resolving OF disguise near armed recruits (active and parked): they must hold
fire. Walk into a base disguised: still no proximity wanted. Get blown at 15 m: OF turns
hostile as before.
