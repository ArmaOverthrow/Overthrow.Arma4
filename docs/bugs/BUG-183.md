## Symptom (player report, 2026-08-18)

Loading a save applies an accounting run: the player receives an income payout ("a bunch of money")
that was already paid before saving. Exploit: wait for an accounting sweep (00/06/12/18h), save, exit,
reload — the same donations/taxes/income land again. Repeatable indefinitely.

## Root cause

`OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.CheckUpdate()` (`Scripts/Game/GameMode/Managers/OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.c:153`)
fires its hour-keyed payloads through once-per-hour **latch fields** declared at `:99-101`:

- income/taxes/donations when `time.m_iHours` ∈ {0,6,12,18} and `m_iHourPaidIncome != hour` (`:171-180`)
- shop restock at hour 7 via `m_iHourPaidStock` (`:183-190`)
- rent at hour 0 via `m_iHourPaidRent` (`:193-201`)

The latches are plain ints, **not persisted** (no serializer field anywhere in
`Scripts/Game/Persistence/`), so they re-initialize to `-1` on load. The in-game clock, however, IS
persisted by the engine (`TimeAndWeatherManagerEntitySerializer`). Save at 12:05 after the noon sweep
→ reload → hour is still 12, latch is `-1` ≠ 12 → `CalculateIncome()` + `UpdateShops()` fire again.
Identical shape for restock (save during hour 7) and rent (save during hour 0).

## Fix

On startup, before the `CheckUpdate` repeat is scheduled (`Init`, `:1279-1291`), initialize all three
latches to the **current** in-game hour instead of `-1`. The landing hour can then never re-pay; the
next genuine boundary pays normally. The only cost is the sub-tick window where the hour flipped ≤10 s
before the save and the sweep had not yet observed it — that payout is skipped, which is the safe
direction (no duplication).

Being fixed alongside `resistance/sleep`, whose time-skip catch-up (`HandleTimeSkip`) must set these
same latches to the landing hour for the same reason — one shared "assert latches = current hour"
helper serves both.

## Acceptance check

1. In an SP campaign, wait for the 12:00 income notification, save, exit, Continue → **no** second
   income payout on load; player money unchanged by the load itself.
2. Save during hour 7 (after restock) and during hour 0 (after rent) → reload triggers no second
   restock / rent charge.
3. Let the clock cross the next 6-hour boundary after loading → income pays exactly once, as normal.

## Resolution (2026-08-19) — FIXED and play-test verified

Fixed alongside `resistance/sleep`, exactly as this report proposed: **assert the three latches to the
current in-game hour** rather than persisting them.

`OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.AssertHourLatches(int hour)` sets `m_iHourPaidIncome`, `m_iHourPaidStock`
and `m_iHourPaidRent` to the given hour, and is called from three places:

1. a **one-shot at the top of `CheckUpdate()`** behind `m_bLatchesAsserted` — the load-order-proof
   point, because the first tick lands ~10 s after `Init` and the persisted clock is certainly restored
   by then. It sits *above* the player-count guard and does not claim the one-shot until the clock read
   actually succeeds;
2. `Init()`, before the `CallLater` — belt and braces, harmless if the clock is not yet restored;
3. `ApplyPersistedEconomy()`, which runs whenever a save payload is applied, **including a live
   re-apply** — a path a persisted-latch fix would not have covered.

`AssertHourLatches` refuses a negative hour, so a failed clock read can never re-arm the very latch the
helper exists to close; the one-shot simply retries on the next tick.

**Verified by the reporter's own three acceptance checks, all green (2026-08-19 play-test):** no second
income payout on load, no second restock or rent charge when saving during hour 7 / hour 0, and income
paying exactly once at the next 6-hour boundary after loading.

The same helper is what makes the sleep time-skip safe — after a skip, all three latches are asserted to
the **landing** hour, which is why the resumed live tick can neither re-fire for the hour it woke in nor
miss the next genuine boundary.

**Accepted cost** (unchanged from the proposal): if the hour flipped in the ≤10 s before the save and the
tick had not yet observed it, that one payout is skipped. Skipping is the safe direction; duplicating was
the exploit.
