## Symptom (server reports, 2026-08-18)

A vehicle parked on top of a vehicle maintenance ramp comes back rotated ~45 degrees after a load.
Sometimes, not always; only reported for vehicles parked on top of objects, never on flat ground.

## Root cause

The pose DATA round-trips exactly — vanilla persistence stores the full `Transform[4]`
(`EntitySerializerDefaultSpawnData`), and Overthrow's registry fallback captures
`GetYawPitchRoll()` and applies it with `Math3D.AnglesToMatrix()`, the same yaw-pitch-roll
convention both ways. Nothing rewrites the rotation on purpose.

What moves the vehicle is physics AT the load. The vehicle self-spawns at its exact saved pose as a
live dynamic body with no saved velocities, while the maintenance ramp under it
(`OVT_VehicleMaintenanceRamp.et`, a buildable in the Overthrow collection) is a separately
self-spawned record — the engine gives no ordering guarantee between collections. If the vehicle's
physics wake before the ramp's collider exists, it free-falls ~0.9 m onto the terrain and settles
rotated; if the ramp then spawns overlapping it, the depenetration impulse kicks it further (the
BUG-129 mechanism). On flat ground the fall height is zero and the settle is symmetric, which is
why only ramp-parked vehicles were reported and why it is intermittent (a spawn-order race).

A second, distinguishable path: `RebuildVehicleFromRecord()` (record unresolvable) also spawns a
dynamic body at the recorded pose, and relocates to nearest parking when the spot reads blocked —
but that path also loses cargo and logs `rebuilt … (contents lost)`, which the reports did not
mention.

## Fix

`OVT_VehicleManagerComponent.ReassertRecordedPose()` (new): compares each registered vehicle's live
pose against its registry record — whose transform `SyncVehicleRecords()` refreshed from the
standing vehicle immediately before the save — and, beyond 0.5 m / 5° drift, zeroes physics
velocities, snaps the vehicle back with `SetTransform()`, and wakes physics (the
`OVT_FlipVehicleAction` idiom). Below tolerance it does nothing, so suspension settle on flat
ground is never touched.

Called from `InitialVehicleCleanup()` (+5 s after load, when every self-spawned entity and its
collision exist), **before** `RegisterPlayerVehicle()` — registration recaptures the record from
the live pose and would otherwise overwrite the saved pose with the drifted one, making the drift
permanent.

Scope: player-registered vehicles (the ones with registry records). Shop/unowned vehicles are not
covered — they have no recorded pose to snap back to.

## Test

`OVT_TEST_PersistenceRoundTrip_VehiclePoseReassert_SnapsBackOnlyBeyondTolerance` — pins both halves
of the seam contract in one frame (below-tolerance drift ignored; 45°/1.7 m drift snapped back
exactly). Can-fail method in the case header; the proving run is owed (this session's environment
could not launch the harness).
