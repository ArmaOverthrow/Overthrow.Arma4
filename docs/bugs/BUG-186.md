Discovered while planning `resistance/sleep` (recorded there as risk **R11**) and deliberately left unfixed — the sleep feature's catch-up replays these steps exactly, so a *slept-through* night is more accurate than a lived-through one.

## Symptom

The occupying faction's resource gain, base spending and threat decay are unreliable overnight. Threat can sit undecayed for hours of game time, and a 6-hour reserve payday can be missed entirely, if the relevant in-game minute falls in a night stretch.

## Root cause

Both periodic managers schedule their tick by dividing a fixed real-time frequency by the **day** acceleration factor, and then gate the work on **exact in-game minute values**:

- `Scripts/Game/GameMode/Managers/Factions/OVT_OccupyingFactionManager.c:306` — `CallLater(CheckUpdate, OF_UPDATE_FREQUENCY / timeMul, true, ...)`, with `OF_UPDATE_FREQUENCY = 60000` (`:170`) and `timeMul` from `GetDayTimeMultiplier()` (`:300`).
- `Scripts/Game/GameMode/Managers/OVT_EconomyManagerComponent.c:1449` — the same shape, `ECONOMY_UPDATE_FREQUENCY = 60000` (`:80`), `timeMul` at `:1433`.

`OVT_TimeAndWeatherHandlerComponent.GetDayTimeMultiplier()` (`Scripts/Game/GameMode/OVT_TimeAndWeatherHandlerComponent.c:9-12`) returns `m_fDayTimeAcceleration` and nothing else. The engine, however, runs day and night at **two different accelerations** — vanilla `SCR_TimeAndWeatherHandlerComponent` swaps `SetDayDuration(DAY_DURATION / m_fNightTimeAcceleration)` in at dusk and `/ m_fDayTimeAcceleration` back in at dawn (`ArmaReforger/scripts/Game/Components/Environment/SCR_TimeAndWeatherHandlerComponent.c:273` and `:281`; the two fields are declared at `:32` and `:35`).

So the tick interval is sized for daytime. At Overthrow's night acceleration (12× vs the day's 6×) each tick advances roughly **two** in-game minutes instead of one, and the gates it has to hit are minute-exact:

- `OVT_OccupyingFactionManager.c:1184` — resource gain requires `time.m_iMinutes == 0`
- `:1211-1214` — threat decay requires `m_iMinutes` ∈ {0, 15, 30, 45}
- `:1196` — the counter-attack roll also requires `m_iMinutes == 0`

A tick that strides from `:14` to `:16` simply never sees `:15`, and the work for that step is lost — not deferred.

## Why the economy is immune

`OVT_EconomyManagerComponent` latches on the **hour** (`m_iHourPaidIncome` / `m_iHourPaidStock` / `m_iHourPaidRent`), so a missed tick is picked up by the next one within the same hour. Only the occupying faction's minute-exact gates lose work. (Those latches have their own defect — see **BUG-183**, being fixed alongside `resistance/sleep`.)

## Suggested fix

Either make the gates catch-up-shaped rather than equality-shaped (track the last processed absolute minute and process every boundary crossed since, the way `resistance/sleep`'s `OVT_SleepSchedule.CountStepCrossings` / `StepMinuteAt` already do), or size the timer off whichever acceleration is currently in force. The first is the more robust of the two — it survives any tick jitter, not just the night case.

## Acceptance check

1. Start an SP campaign and let the clock run through a full night with the console visible.
2. Count `Reduced Threat to:` lines per in-game hour across dusk → dawn: today they thin out or stop; after the fix there should be four per in-game hour throughout.
3. Confirm a `Gaining Resources` / `Reserve Resources` pair still lands on each 6-hour boundary that falls at night.
