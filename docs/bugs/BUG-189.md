> ID note: fixed on main 2026-08-20 (commits c26a5841 + 2e0ce7df), where the session numbered it BUG-185 locally; filed as BUG-189 at merge time — v1.5/tracker BUG-185 is the visibility half this bug follows on from.

## Symptom

Vehicles could spawn where an offline player's (invisible, reserved) vehicle was parked — cars spawned INSIDE the invisible ones. Players could also drive straight into an invisible obstacle.

## Root cause

The BUG-086/BUG-185 reservation model hides entities via entity flags (VISIBLE/TRACEABLE/ACTIVE), but none of those flags unregisters the rigid body from the physics world — a sleeping body still collides. A reserved vehicle was a car you could not see, shoot or use, but drove straight into: on clients from the moment BUG-185's fix hid it there too, and on the authority machine (SP, listen host, server-side AI traffic) all along. Parking searches also treated the space as free-but-blocked.

## Fix

`OVT_ReservationSyncComponent.ApplyPhysicsState()` — the collision half: zeroes the body's interaction layer (saved and restored per machine) and `SetActive(INACTIVE)` keeps the freed body from being simulated; `Release()` restores the exact saved layer. `OVT_ParkingComponent` asks the reservation before treating a spot as occupied.

## Test

`OVT_TEST_Init_Persistence_ReservationReplicatesToClients` pins the collision half: reserve → interaction layer 0, release → the exact saved layer restored, with a vacuity guard on the pre-reservation layer.
