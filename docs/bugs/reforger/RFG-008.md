## Summary

The vehicle user actions apply their hostile-faction check inconsistently. The door and handbrake actions explicitly allow same-faction use even when the faction reads as an enemy of itself, but `SCR_GetInUserAction` and `SCR_RemoveCasualtyUserAction` omit the `vehicleFaction != characterFaction` term. In any state where a faction's relation to itself evaluates hostile — observed client-side while the relation table is empty/not yet replicated, and by design in FFA-style setups — a player can open the doors of their own faction's vehicle and toggle its handbrake, but cannot get in or remove a casualty ("Seat hostile").

## Script citations (1.7.0.54)

Guarded (same-faction never blocks):

- `scripts/Game/UserActions/SCR_OpenVehicleDoorUserAction.c:89` — `… IsFactionEnemy(vehicleFaction) && vehicleFaction != characterFaction`
- `scripts/Game/UserActions/SCR_CloseVehicleDoorUserAction.c:89` — same
- `scripts/Game/UserActions/SCR_ToggleHandbrakeAction.c:54` — same

Unguarded (same faction can read hostile):

- `scripts/Game/UserActions/SCR_GetInUserAction.c:93` — `if (characterFaction && vehicleFaction && characterFaction.IsFactionEnemy(vehicleFaction))`
- `scripts/Game/UserActions/SCR_RemoveCasualtyUserAction.c:83` — same shape

## Steps to reproduce

Easiest deterministic form: a scenario where a faction is (even transiently) enemy to itself — e.g. a client whose faction-relation data has not arrived yet, or an FFA configuration. Approach an own-faction vehicle: doors open fine, Get In shows `#AR-UserAction_SeatHostile`.

## Expected

All five actions use the same predicate; own-faction vehicles are always enterable wherever their doors are already operable.

## Workaround used in Overthrow

`Scripts/Game/UserActions/Modded/SCR_GetInUserAction.c` re-adds the `vehicleFaction != characterFaction` term to `CanBePerformedScript`.
