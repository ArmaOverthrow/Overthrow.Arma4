# Player Groups — Requirements

**Epic:** core
**Created:** 2026-08-06
**Addresses:** GitHub issue **#147** (second half — "Cant manage Group menu"), and the multiplayer play-test of 2026-08-06 that produced **BUG-088**

## Overview

Overthrow gives every player a one-man group at spawn and nothing supports leaving it, so players cannot squad up with friends. This feature makes that group **private-by-default and yours**, and makes joining someone else's group a **deliberate, warned, reversible** act — with the recruit consequences spelled out before you commit.

The governing rule: **recruits are owned by a player, not by a group.** They follow their owner into whatever group the owner is in; while they are there, that group's leader can command them (vanilla behaviour — the leader commands the group's slave group). Ownership never transfers: dismiss, rename, inventory and loadout stay with the owner, and the recruits come back with the owner when they leave, switch groups or reconnect.

Vanilla already ships the mechanics — private groups (`SCR_AIGroup.SetPrivate`), request-to-join with leader approval (`PlayerRequestToJoinPrivateGroup` → `AddRequester` → `SCR_RequestToJoinSubMenu`/`SCR_JoinRequestEntry`), and per-group slave groups for AI. This feature is mostly **wiring, a UX layer over the vanilla Group Menu, and the recruit-follows-owner rule**.

**Precondition:** the BUG-088 fix (players actually get a faction, a group and leadership in a replicated session). Nothing here is testable before that lands.

## Requirements

### Your own group, by default

- On connect/spawn every player is in **their own group**, is its **leader**, and the group is named after them (`OVT_SpawnLogic.CreateAndJoinGroup` already does the naming).
- That group is created **private** (`SCR_AIGroup.SetPrivate(true)`), so another player must *request* to join and the owner must approve. A player is never silently added to someone else's group.
- Leaving a shared group returns the player to their own group; if it no longer exists it is recreated. There must be **no state in which a player has no group** — that is the BUG-088 failure mode (no commanding, no recruits).
- The group cap is vanilla's `m_iMaxMembers` (**6** on `Prefabs/Groups/PlayableGroup.et`) and applies to players only; recruits live in the slave group and do not consume player slots.

### Joining another player's group

- Joining goes through vanilla's private-group request flow, server-authoritative throughout: the requester asks, the **leader approves**, the server performs the membership change (`RPC_AskJoinGroup` → `MovePlayerToGroup`). Overthrow reacts to the result; it must not perform membership changes client-side.
- The approving leader sees **who** is asking and **how many recruits they would bring**.
- A join that the server rejects (group full, faction mismatch, group gone) must surface a message rather than failing silently.

### Group Menu overrides (the UX layer)

- The vanilla Group Menu gains **explanatory text** stating the Overthrow model: you start in your own group; joining another player's group hands command of your recruits to that group's leader for as long as you are in it.
- **Confirmation dialog before joining another player's group**, following the project's existing pattern (`SCR_ConfigurableDialogUi.CreateFromPreset` with an Overthrow dialog-preset config — see `OVT_CampMenuContext` `DELETE_CAMP`). The join is only requested on confirm. Content:
  - what changes: *"<Leader> will be able to command your recruits while you are in their group."*
  - the recruit clause, **only when the player has recruits**, naming the count: *"Your N recruits will move to <Leader>'s group and take orders from <Leader>. You still own them — they return to you when you leave the group, change groups, or reconnect."*
  - a plain cancel path.
- The single client-side seam is `SCR_GroupSubMenuBase`'s join action (it funnels everything through `m_PlayerGroupController.RequestJoinGroup(GetSelectedGroupID())`), so a `modded class` override there covers both the button and any other route into it. Confirm during planning that the same seam covers the tile button (`SCR_GroupTileButton`) and gamepad navigation.
- All new user-facing strings are `#OVT-` keys added to `Language/localization_Overthrow.st` (the editable master); the runtime `.conf` exports are regenerated by the user in Workbench — never hand-edited. Until an export exists, layouts use literal text.
- Gamepad/console usable: no mouse-only affordances, dialog confirm/cancel reachable on a controller (see the `overthrow-ui-patterns` skill).

### Recruit transfer (server-authoritative)

- **Ownership is never rewritten by grouping.** `OVT_RecruitData.m_sOwnerPersistentId` and `OVT_PlayerOwnerComponent` are untouched by any group operation.
- When a player's group membership changes, the server moves **every online recruit that player owns** into the new group's slave group (`SCR_PlayerControllerGroupComponent.AddAIToSlaveGroup`, the same call the recruit path now uses). The hooks are the static invokers `SCR_AIGroup.GetOnPlayerAdded()` / `GetOnPlayerRemoved()`, which fire on the server for every membership change regardless of what caused it.
- Recruits recruited **while** in a shared group join that shared group (they follow the owner's current group, not the owner's own group).
- Commanding: while in a shared group the leader commands all recruits in the slave group. That is vanilla and needs no Overthrow code beyond placing the agents.
- **Owner-only actions stay owner-only in every group**: dismiss, rename, open-inventory (`OVT_OpenInventoryCommand`), loadout apply, show-on-map. Kill XP credits the recruit's owner, never the group leader.
- **A departing owner takes their recruits with them.** When the owner leaves the group, disconnects, or is otherwise removed, none of their recruits may remain in the ex-group's slave group — otherwise the ex-leader keeps commanding someone else's AI. Offline owners' recruits follow the existing offline flow (bodies reserved/despawned per the BUG-086 model).

### Edge cases the design must answer

- Leader disconnects or is promoted away (`CheckForLeader` promotes the next player): recruits stay with their owner and remain in the group; the new leader commands them.
- Two members of a shared group each recruit up to the 16-recruit cap: a six-player group could reach ~96 AI in one slave group. Measure it; if it is a problem, cap per-group AI or warn at a threshold rather than failing silently.
- A recruit whose owner is offline must not be commandable by the group.
- Group emptied / deleted while a member is mid-join.
- Player joins, recruits, then leaves in quick succession (agents mid-move).

### Testing

- **Logic tier (world-free, UI-free):** the "which recruits move" mapping — given an owner persistent id and a target group, the set of recruit ids to transfer — implemented in a helper so it can be pinned, including the offline-recruit exclusion and the cap arithmetic. Each new case must be proven able to fail before shipping.
- **Manual, two clients (the only way — MP is uncovered):** request/approve join; leader commands the other player's recruits; owner-only actions refused to the leader; owner leaves → recruits follow; owner disconnects/reconnects → recruits return to the owner; leader disconnects → promotion path; join a full group.

## Open questions (settle during planning)

1. **Reconnect:** does a player who joined a friend's group come back to *their own* group or to the friend's group? Recommendation: **their own** — it is predictable, it matches "your group is yours", and vanilla's rejoin path (`SCR_GroupsManagerComponent.RejoinPlayer`, which itself calls the client-request wrapper from server code) is not currently reliable in Overthrow. Either way the recruit rule is unchanged: they follow the owner.
2. **Invites as well as requests?** Vanilla also supports the leader inviting a player. Cheap to enable; decide whether both directions are in scope.
3. Should the *leader* be warned when someone joins with recruits (a notification, not a dialog)?
4. Does anything besides recruits key off "same group" in Overthrow (job assignment, notifications, map icons) that changes meaning once groups can be shared?

## Out of Scope

- Changing recruit **ownership** (gifting/trading a recruit to another player).
- Squad roles, group loadout presets, group flags/callsign customisation.
- Group chat, radio frequency or map-marker rework.
- Garrison or officer command structures (`resistance/core`).
- AI-only groups and the occupying faction's group handling.
