## Verdict definitions

- **CONFIG-PIN**: the case reads shipped data and asserts a specific value or order from it. It breaks when a designer tunes the config.
- **WIRING**: the case checks that a component or manager exists. No tuned value.
- **WALL-CLOCK**: the case waits real time or a bounded poll count.
- **LOGIC**: pure logic on test-authored inputs.
- **DUPLICATE**: the case repeats another case in the same suite.

## Actions

- **DELETE**: remove the case.
- **TRIM**: keep the structural check (every entry has a cost, no two entries share a prefab, every buildable prefab is destructible). Remove the literal values and the counts.
- **REFIXTURE**: keep the persistence round trip. Pick the fixture as the first registry entry, not by the name "Town Patrol".
- **MOVE TO SOAK**: move the case to `OVT_TEST_SoakSuite`, a new Init-tier suite in the All group only.
- **KEEP**: no change.
