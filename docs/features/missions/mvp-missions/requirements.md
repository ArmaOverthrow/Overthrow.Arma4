# MVP Missions — Requirements

**Epic:** missions
**Created:** 2026-08-13

## Overview

The release gate of the missions epic: ship mission configs reaching near-parity with the 7 legacy jobs plus a few showcase missions that demonstrate what the new system can do (branching, co-op rewards, mission items) — and, in the same feature, **completely remove the legacy Jobs system**. Parity and teardown are verified together.

## Requirements

- **Parity set** — mission configs covering the behaviour of the 7 shipped jobs (see `Configs/Jobs/*.conf` while they still exist):
  1. assassinate-traitor — kill a collaborator hiding in a town house, guarded by OF infantry
  2. base-recon — scout an enemy base by getting within 100 m
  3. raise-support — push a town's support from 0 to 10%
  4. propaganda-run — put up posters in a low-support town
  5. pirate-radio — build a pirate radio in a town that has none
  6. sabotage-radio-tower — knock out / capture the enemy radio tower covering a town
  7. assassinate-officer — kill the officer at an occupied base to swing nearby towns' support
- **Showcase missions** — 1–3 new missions that exercise branching paths, multi-participant reward distribution, and world-placed mission items (e.g. steal-and-deliver documents), proving capabilities jobs never had.
- **Jobs teardown** (same feature, after parity is play-verified): delete `OVT_JobManagerComponent`, `Systems/Jobs/` framework classes, `Configs/Jobs/`, `OVT_JobsContext` + `JobsMenu.layout` + `OVT_JobListEntryHandler`, `OVT_JobManagerSerializer` + its `Configs/Systems/Persistence/Overthrow.conf` entry, the `AcceptJob`/`DeclineJob` RPCs on `OVT_PlayerCommsComponent`, the job half of `OVT_MapLocationWaypoint`, `OVT_Global.GetJobs()`, and the job prefab wiring on `OVT_OverthrowGameMode.et`.
- Verify old saves load cleanly after teardown — orphaned job payloads should be skipped as unclaimed component data (confirm with the persistence-forensics skill, don't assume).
- Replace the job test suites with mission equivalents of at least the same coverage (logic maths, init config-resolve + stable-id uniqueness, persistence round trip).
- Retire `OVT-Job_*` strings via `Language/localization_Overthrow.st` only; runtime exports are regenerated by the user.

## Dependencies

- `missions/framework` and `missions/mission-ui` complete — content is authored against the finished module library and player loop.

## Out of Scope

- Officer-created missions — `missions/resistance-missions`.
- Migrating in-flight legacy jobs into missions — old job save payloads are orphaned, not converted.
- Expanding the module library beyond what parity + showcase missions need.
