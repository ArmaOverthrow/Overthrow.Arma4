## Purpose

The resistance epic owns the player's side of Overthrow's war: the resistance faction manager (player faction choice, camps, FOBs, garrisons, officers), the base building and placement system (placeables, buildables, build/place UI), the recruit system (recruiting and managing AI squadmates), and the loadout system (saving/loading player and officer equipment). Together these are the tools the player uses to grow from a lone dissident into an armed movement.
